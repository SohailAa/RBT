01 / The Gaps in Existing ABA Software

6 Critical Areas Where MeasurePM & Legacy Apps "Do Not Cover It All"

Therapists in the field told us what they dread most about other software: juggling physical velcro boards while tapping a tablet, typing 15-page insurance progress reports by hand, and struggling with app syncing freezes. Here is how EBA solves each one.

🧸

1. No In-Session Kid Engagement

In MeasurePM, therapists must carry separate physical velcro token boards, dinosaur eggs, and plastic star charts while juggling a tablet during therapy.

❌ MeasurePM: Rigid numbers screen; therapist juggles physical velcro toys.
✓ EBA Kid Mode: Interactive Space Rocket, Dino Island, LEGO & Train token boards with automated Web Audio cheer praise right on screen!
🎲

2. Missing Play-Based NET Multi-Target Grid

Capturing natural environment teaching (NET) during play in traditional apps requires frustrating screen transitions and back-and-forth menu clicks.

❌ MeasurePM: Linear trial-by-trial lists unfit for fast-moving naturalistic play.
✓ EBA NET Grid: High-density card grid displaying all active targets simultaneously with 1-tap rapid prompt chips.
📄

3. The 4–8 Hour Insurance Re-Auth Nightmare

Every 6 months, supervising BCBAs spend endless unpaid nights copying graph screenshots into Microsoft Word to justify medical necessity for insurance payers.

❌ MeasurePM: Raw CSV graphs; clinician still writes 15-page clinical packets manually.
✓ EBA 1-Click Packet: Auto-synthesizes all 5 BACB & insurance sections (CPT 97151/97155) with exact baseline vs post-BIP trajectory and CPT justifications!
📈

4. No Hourly FBA Scatterplot & Peak Trigger Radar

BCBAs need to identify ecological patterns and setting event clusters across the child's daily schedule (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).

❌ MeasurePM: Simple daily tally bars without hourly temporal clustering.
✓ EBA Hourly Scatterplot: Full 8 AM–5 PM heatmap that automatically detects peak disruption windows (e.g. 10 AM transition demand) with proactive BIP alerts!
🤖

5. AI Without Guardrails vs. Hard BCBA Locks

Generic AI wrappers hallucinate clinical advice, violating BACB ethics and creating severe malpractice and insurance audit liabilities.

❌ MeasurePM: Zero native generative AI tools.
✓ EBA Clinical Guardrails: Deterministic AI creates child-tailored Social Stories (with audio read-aloud) and FCT plans—permanently locked as drafts until approved by a BCBA!
🏢

6. No Dedicated 4-Persona Command Decks

A practice owner wants to see gross revenue and CPT code yield; a BCBA wants target mastery alerts; an RBT wants today's tasks; an admin wants delinquent notes.

❌ MeasurePM: One-size-fits-all administrative view without role-specific command decks.
✓ EBA 4-Persona Switcher: Dedicated command decks for Practice Owners (P&L), BCBAs (clinical velocity), RBTs (shift tasks & 5% tracker), and Admins (missing notes & claims).
