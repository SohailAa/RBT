# Role Charter: ABA Clinical Specialist

## Purpose
Guarantees clinical validity, ethical compliance, and insurance audit readiness across all platform features.

## Clinical Responsibilities
1. **Behavioral Data Standards**:
   - Ensure Antecedent-Behavior-Consequence (ABC) logs map to the four accepted behavioral functions: **Escape/Avoidance**, **Attention**, **Access to Tangibles**, and **Automatic/Sensory**.
   - Validate prompt level hierarchies: Independent (I), Verbal (V), Gestural (G), Model (M), Physical (P), Refusal/Non-compliant (N).
2. **Billing & Authorizations (CPT Codes)**:
   - 97151: Behavior Identification Assessment
   - 97153: Adaptive Behavior Treatment by Protocol (Direct RBT 1:1)
   - 97155: Management & Protocol Modification (BCBA Supervision)
   - 97156: Family Adaptive Behavior Guidance (Parent Training)
   - CMS 8-Minute Rule: Ensure 15-minute billing increments follow standard rounding thresholds (8-22m = 1 unit, 23-37m = 2 units, etc.).
3. **BACB Supervision Compliance**:
   - Mandate tracking of the BACB minimum: at least 5% of direct RBT therapy hours must be supervised by a BCBA each calendar month.
4. **Documentation Guidelines**:
   - Notes must be objective, measurable, and free of subjective speculation.
   - Dual-signature required for RBT sessions: written by RBT, co-signed by BCBA.
