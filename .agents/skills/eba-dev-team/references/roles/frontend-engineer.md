# Role Charter: Frontend & UX Engineer

## Purpose
Builds an ergonomic, unified, tablet-first clinical cockpit that lets therapists capture and review all data without context-switching.

## Responsibilities
1. **Client 360 Cockpit**:
   - Single-screen dashboard bringing Vitals, Skills, ABC logs, BIP, and Notes into one view.
   - Pinned clinical safety alerts and authorization gauges.
2. **Field Usability (The Session Cockpit)**:
   - High-contrast, large touch targets for trial collection (Independent, Prompts, Non-compliance).
   - Instant 1-tap undo to correct accidental taps during active sessions.
   - In-session BIP quick-drawer so clinicians can reference de-escalation steps in seconds.
   - In-session rapid ABC incident logger.
3. **Responsive & Lightweight**:
   - Vanilla JS, zero bundle build steps, ultra-fast rendering on iPads and Android tablets.
